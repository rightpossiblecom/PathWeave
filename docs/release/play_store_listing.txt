TITLE
PathWeave

SHORT DESCRIPTION (76 chars)
Calm single-stroke logic puzzles. Weave one unbroken path across every tile.

FULL DESCRIPTION
PathWeave is a clean, tactile spatial logic puzzle game built for quiet focus and satisfying deduction.

Your goal on every board is simple to learn and deeply rewarding to master: draw a single continuous ribbon starting from the glowing origin tile that visits every open tile on the grid without crossing your own trail or stepping onto dark stone blocks.

As you progress from gentle 4x4 warmups into larger 6x6 and 7x6 boards, numbered step anchors introduce an extra layer of crisp forward planning. An anchor marked with a step number must be reached on that exact move count, turning every puzzle into a pure logical deduction with zero guesswork.

KEY FEATURES
- 40 Handcrafted Campaign Puzzles organized across four chapters: Breeze, Weave, Labyrinth, and Mastery.
- Daily Weave Mode delivering a fresh, deterministically generated 6x5 puzzle board every single day with local streak tracking.
- Smooth Single-Stroke Controls supporting both continuous finger dragging and crisp tile tapping, plus effortless drag-back unwinding.
- Smart Prefix Hints that gently restore any diverged trail back onto the true solution path and reveal the next step whenever you get stuck.
- Optional Step Counter Overlay to visualize exact move numbers across complex boards.
- Adaptive Light and Dark Themes crafted with Material 3 color harmony and subtle haptic feedback.
- Complete Offline Play with no login, no ads, no timers forcing mistakes, and zero personal data collection.

# Privacy Policy for PathWeave

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Application Name:** PathWeave  
**Package Identifier:** `com.pathweave.app`

## 1. Overview
PathWeave is an offline spatial logic puzzle game. We respect your privacy completely: **we do not collect any personal data**, we do not require registration or user accounts, and we do not include advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or process any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, IP address, or location) or usage analytics.

## 3. Local On-Device Storage
PathWeave stores your gameplay progress—including solved puzzle levels, star ratings, daily puzzle completion dates, theme selection, and haptic preferences—exclusively on your device using local on-device storage.
- This data never leaves your device.
- You can erase all saved game data at any time by opening **Settings -> Clear App Data** inside the app or by uninstalling the application.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
Because PathWeave collects zero user data, no data is shared with, disclosed to, or sold to any third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy
PathWeave contains family-friendly geometric logic puzzles and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If future updates to PathWeave change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly with a revised effective date.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at `support@pathweave.app`.
